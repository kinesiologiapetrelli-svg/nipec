7.3Theft, fire and events. In the event of theft, robbery, fire or natural event, the Borrower shall file a report with the authorities within 24 hours and send a copy to the Lender within 48 hours. In the event of total loss or irreparable damage not attributable to the Lender, the replacement value stated in clause 1.1 is due, less any amount paid by insurance.

7.4The Lender is liable, under applicable law, for pre-existing, hidden or manufacturing defects not caused by the Borrower, and for deficiencies in the documentation it is required to supply. Fair wear and tear is not recoverable damage.

7.5In the event of a defect the Parties document the condition and, if the cause is disputed, obtain an assessment from an authorised technician or an independent expert accepted by both. Costs are borne by the Party responsible according to the established cause; failing such a finding, the Parties agree in writing before any work is carried out.
