How it works

It isn't electrostimulation.

The focused electromagnetic field passes through the skin and fatty tissue and directly stimulates the deep motor neurons: the muscle contracts supramaximally, at a rate normal exercise cannot reach. The device is non-ionising, athermal and acts only on motor neurons, not on sensory ones.

The sessions

How long, how often.

30 · 45 · 60 minutes

Standard, intensive, maximal session. At most two treatments a day: upper and lower body.

2 a week, for a month

This is the optimal protocol given in the manual. Then one maintenance session a month.

5 modes per area

HIIT, strength, muscle development, body shaping, fat burning: power and frequencies already set for arms, calves, thighs, glutes and abdomen.

When not to use it

Absolute contraindications.

Pacemaker, defibrillator and other active electronic implants, neurostimulators and cochlear implant · pregnancy · active cancer · epilepsy · active infections and high fever · deep vein thrombosis · active bleeding · serious heart disease · recent stroke or heart attack. Other conditions need a case-by-case assessment: the full list is in the user manual, which we deliver with the device.
