The NIPEC method

A process that doesn't stop at delivery.

The device is just the beginning. What keeps a centre working is what comes after: the protocols, a trained operator, someone who picks up the phone. That's why we work in four stages.

01

Analysis

Before talking about devices, we look at your centre: who your clients are, how many treatment rooms you have, what an hour of work costs, what you already sell.

  • Questionnaire and visit (or video call)
  • Review of your price list and diary
  • Where the margin you lose today sits
02

Design

Together we choose the right technology and build the treatment around it: prices, packages, session length, purchase formula.

  • Device and configuration
  • Treatment price list and packages
  • Purchase or rental, with the numbers on the table
03

Training

Hands-on delivery in the treatment room and a staged path that ends with an exam. The operator leaves with a certificate valid for 12 months.

  • Day 0 in the room, +7 follow-up, +21 online
  • Exam at +45 days
  • Protocols and rule cards, in Italian and Albanian
04

Ongoing support

After delivery we stay. A single contact for your centre, direct assistance and a monthly check to prevent device downtime.

  • 10-minute monthly video call
  • Parts replacement and logistics
  • Launch marketing campaign
