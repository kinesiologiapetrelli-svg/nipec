CERTIFIED TRAINING

Not a one-day course. A path.

After a single day people remember half. So NIPEC training runs in stages and ends with an exam: a certified operator works better and the machine lasts longer.

  1. Day 0 Hands-on delivery Installation and first guided sessions in the room.
  2. +7 days First follow-up Bad habits get fixed before they become faults.
  3. +21 days Online session Advanced protocols and questions on real cases.
  4. +45 days Exam Practical test and written test.
