The NIPEC method

A process that doesn’t stop at delivery.

The ring in our logo is open: it’s an orbit, a continuous process. The moving dot is us, going forward with you. The three rising bars are your salon growing.

01

Analysis

Before talking about machines we look at your salon: who your clients are, how many rooms you have, what an hour of work costs, what you already sell.

  • Questionnaire and visit, or video call
  • Review of price list and diary
  • Where the margin you lose today is
02

Design

Together we choose the right technology and build the treatment around it: prices, packages, session length, purchase formula.

  • Device and configuration
  • Treatment menu and packages
  • Purchase or rental, with the numbers on the table
03

Training

Hands-on delivery in the room and a staged path that ends with an exam. Your operator comes out with a certificate valid for 12 months.

  • Day 0 in the room, +7 follow-up, +21 online
  • Exam at +45 days
  • Protocols and rule cards, in Italian and Albanian
04

Ongoing support

After delivery we stay. One contact person for your salon, direct assistance and a monthly check to prevent downtime.

  • A 10-minute monthly video call
  • Parts replacement and logistics
  • Launch marketing campaign

Our view

If a machine breaks, the cause usually starts with us.

One day of training, then nobody. The operator remembers half the rules, the machine gets misused, a fault appears a few months later. So we changed how we deliver: staged training, a certified operator, one contact per salon and a check every month.

Phase 1 is free.

We start by analysing your salon. If you don’t need a new machine, we’ll tell you.
