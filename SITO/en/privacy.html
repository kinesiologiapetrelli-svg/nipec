Privacy and cookie policy

Last updated: October 2026

Data controller

PHD System Srl, Via Rovetta 29, 25080 Padenghe sul Garda (BS), Italy — VAT IT04696530981. For any request about your data: info@nipecestetica.com.

What data we collect

Only what you give us in the contact form: name, phone and, optionally, email, salon name, city, interest and message. If you write to us on WhatsApp or by email, we process the data in that conversation.

Why we use it and on what basis

To answer your request and prepare a proposal. The legal basis is taking pre-contractual steps at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We don’t use your data for newsletters or advertising without your separate consent.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months from the last contact if you don’t become a customer. If you do, for as long as accounting and tax rules require.

Who we share it with

With the provider that hosts the site and receives the form (Netlify Inc., USA, participating in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) and, if your request needs it, with the NIPEC office in Tirana. We never sell data.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data and object to processing by writing to info@nipecestetica.com. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it).

Cookies

This site uses no profiling cookies or analytics. Fonts are hosted on our own server, so your visit isn’t shared with third parties. That’s why we don’t ask for cookie consent.
