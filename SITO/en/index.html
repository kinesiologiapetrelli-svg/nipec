FAQ

The questions everyone asks.

Can’t find yours? Message us on WhatsApp: we reply within one working day.

Is it better to buy or rent?

It depends on how busy your diary already is and how much you want to commit at the start. In the consultation we run the numbers on both options with you, before you sign.

How long is the training?

It’s a path, not a day: hands-on delivery on day 0, a follow-up at 7 days, an online session at 21, the exam at 45. The operator certificate is valid for 12 months.

What happens if the machine stops?

You get direct support, and the 12-month warranty covers parts replacement and logistics. With the monthly check we aim to catch problems before downtime.

Are the machines certified?

Yes. Our lasers carry Italian CE marking and already comply with the EU MDR regulation. Every device comes with traceable documents and serial number.

Do you also work in Albania?

Yes. We have an office in Tirana, protocols and training in Albanian, and support on +355 69 212 9786.
