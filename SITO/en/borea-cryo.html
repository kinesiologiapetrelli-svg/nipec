How it works

Losing weight and reducing fat are not the same thing.

When you lose weight, fat cells shrink but stay. With cryolipolysis, controlled cooling between −10 and +5 °C under the skin reduces the number of cells in the treated area. The body clears them on its own over the following weeks.

Session

Applicator on the area, 360° cooling over the whole surface.

1–3 months

Treated cells are cleared through the lymphatic system.
