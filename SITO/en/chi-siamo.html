About us

Technology, method and business vision for professional aesthetics.

NIPEC is PHD System’s aesthetic technology brand. We work with beauty salons in Italy and Albania, and our job isn’t delivering machines: it’s making the salon that uses them work.

The founder

Giorgio Petrelli

He comes from physiotherapy and kinesiology, with years of work in international spas. He knows the salon from the treatment room side, not just the supplier’s. Hence the idea behind NIPEC: give salons not a device, but a system to make it pay.
